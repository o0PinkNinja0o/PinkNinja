Lore

She was born with an extra set of ears; her family, the Endermen, didn't know if it was a curse or a blessing. Not knowing what to do with her, the Endermen left her alone most of her life. Not quite abandoning her, but ignoring her most of the time. With all this free time, she grew to love exploring and wandering off- not like her family cared. This grew her adventurous spirit.

Current story

As soon as she was old enough to go out on her own, she did. She didn't look back as she dived into the unknown. One day, though, she found a shy Shulker too scared to adventure alone. So, a friendship was formed. Who knows what they will find in The End, the secrets hidden, the many places and adventures they will go on?
